# Quantum Math Foundations

Mathematical foundations of quantum computing — built from first principles.

**Audience**: Anyone with a technical background (engineering, physics, CS) who wants to understand the math behind quantum computing at a rigorous level — not a pop-science overview.

**Approach**: Every concept is motivated before it is defined. Every definition is followed by worked examples. Nothing is introduced without a reason to care about it.

**Goal**: Reach the point where quantum algorithms (Shor, Grover) and quantum circuit notation are fully legible — not as black boxes, but as direct consequences of the math.

## How lessons are structured

```
1. MOTIVATION      — why does this problem exist?
2. INTUITION       — geometric or physical picture
3. FORMAL DEF      — precise definition
4. WORKED EXAMPLES — step by step, nothing skipped
5. COMMON TRAPS    — what breaks and why
6. EXERCISES       — 3 problems, easy → hard
7. NEXT LESSON     — what this feeds into
```
